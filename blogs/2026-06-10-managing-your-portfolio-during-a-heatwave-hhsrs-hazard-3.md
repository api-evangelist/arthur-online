While the weather may have cooled down for now, it won’t be too long until we can expect another heatwave. For property managers and landlords, spiking temperatures mean uncomfortable tenants and major compliance risk. Excess heat is a Category 1 hazard under the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (HHSRS).
